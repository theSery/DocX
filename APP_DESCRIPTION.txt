DocX — Application Descriptions
================================

SHORT DESCRIPTION
-----------------
DocX helps you create and submit legally structured applications, complaints, and other documents in just three steps — choose a template, fill in the details, and send it to the recipient directly from the app.


LONG DESCRIPTION
----------------
DocX is a mobile legal-document assistant for individuals and businesses in Armenia. It makes it easy to prepare and submit complaints and applications against administrative acts — including traffic and patrol fines, camera-detected violations, Yerevan municipality acts, and tax (ՊԵԿ) acts — without drafting documents from scratch.

How it works:
1. Choose the template you need from ready-made legal categories and issues.
2. Fill in the required details through a guided form.
3. Generate a formatted PDF, add your signature if needed, and send it to the recipient right from the app.

Key features:
• Ready legal templates for common administrative complaints and applications
• Guided document creation with PDF generation
• Electronic signature support
• Document history with search, filters, download, and share
• Personal file storage for supporting documents
• Secure access with PIN and Face ID / Touch ID
• In-app wallet for document generation

With DocX, appealing fines and administrative acts becomes faster, clearer, and more accessible — in just a few taps.
